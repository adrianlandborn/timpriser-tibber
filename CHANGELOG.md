# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial Connect IQ Store release: Tibber prices with network fees and energy tax, price chart, glance, Swedish/English/Norwegian/German/Dutch.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

- Support for Norwegian/German/Dutch and differnt currencies. Taxes are only enabled for Swedish contracts.
- Defaults (price type, price interval, grid fees, energy tax) can be set from the Garmin Connect app; the last change from phone or watch wins.
- Older touch-only devices (vivoactive 4, venu): a tap scrolls through the screens.
