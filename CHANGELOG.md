# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial Connect IQ Store release: Tibber prices with network fees and energy tax, price chart, glance, Swedish/English/Norwegian/German/Dutch.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

- Support for Norwegian/German/Dutch and different currencies. Taxes are only enabled for Swedish contracts.
- Defaults (price type, price interval, grid fees, energy tax) can be set from the Garmin Connect app; the last change from phone or watch wins.
- Older touch-only devices (Vivoactive 3 Music, Vivoactive 4/4S, Venu, Venu Sq Music and the Legacy Marvel/Star Wars editions): a tap scrolls through the screens.
- Support for about 50 more devices: Forerunner 245 Music, 255, 645 Music, 745, 57x (42/47 mm), 945, 945 LTE, 955, 965 and 970; Fenix 5 Plus family and Fenix E; Descent G2, Mk2, Mk2S and Mk3; Enduro 3; Epix Pro (Gen 2) 51 mm; Instinct 3 AMOLED and Crossover AMOLED; Marq (Adventurer, Athlete, Aviator, Captain, Commander, Driver, Expedition, Golfer); Legacy Marvel/Star Wars editions; Venu 2, 2 Plus, 2S, 4 41/45 mm, Sq 2, Sq 2 Music, Sq Music and X1; Vivoactive 3 Music; Approach S50, S70 42/47 mm; D2 Air X10.
- Fixed the glance showing empty boxes instead of the "Open Tibber" text when no price is cached.
